A Bible app built for one small group, not an audience

No public feed. No followers. No algorithm.

Most Bible apps quietly want the same thing every other app wants: an audience. More followers, more feed, more time-on-app. devo wants the opposite. Your small group of up to 12 close friends, and nothing bigger. The few people who actually know you, reading the Bible alongside you.

Why a public feed ruins a Bible app

Make your reflections public and you start writing for strangers. The honesty drains out and the performance creeps in. A small, closed group is the only place you’ll write what you actually think God is showing you, because it’s only going to the people you trust with it.

Up to 12 people you actually know

Your group is capped at twelve on purpose. No strangers, no “people you may know,” no growing it forever. Small enough that every reflection is for someone specific, not for a crowd.

No follower count, no algorithm, no discovery tab

There’s no number to chase and nothing deciding what you see. No leaderboard, no ranking, no feed optimizing for outrage or envy. Just your group’s reflections, in order, when they post them.

Post to unlock: write before you read

You can’t lurk. Read today’s verse, write your own reflection, and that’s what unlocks your friends’. Everyone shows up, or no one sees anything. That’s the post-first devotional idea devo is built on.

The shared tree your whole group grows

Your streak is a little tree that grows on the days your group shows up. Miss one day, it’s fine. Miss two in a row and it falls. It’s kept in your past trees, remembered, not erased. A forgiving streak you grow together, not a number you grind alone.

Free, no ads, no paywall

devo is free. No ads, no subscription, nothing to upgrade. If that ever changes, you’ll hear it from the founder first, not from a paywall.
