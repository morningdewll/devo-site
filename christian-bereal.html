What people mean by “a Christian BeReal”

Usually three things: no performing for strangers, a daily nudge to actually show up, and only seeing your friends once you’ve shown up yourself. That last part is the point. You can’t lurk. You give before you get.

devo is built on exactly that. Read today’s verse, write what you noticed, and your friends’ reflections unlock.

How devo is different from BeReal

  • BeReal: a dual-camera photo of your random Tuesday.
    devo: your reflection on today’s Bible verse.
  • BeReal: the post disappears.
    devo: your reflections stay, and your tree keeps your history.
  • BeReal: a wide net of acquaintances.
    devo: a small group of up to 12 close friends.
  • Both: you post your own first, then you read everyone else’s.
    devo calls it post to unlock. The gate is the point.

The tree, instead of a streak number

devo has a streak, but it’s a little tree that grows on the days you and your small group show up. Miss one day, it’s fine. Miss two in a row and it falls, but it doesn’t vanish to zero. The old tree gets planted in your past trees, so the season you had is remembered, not erased.
