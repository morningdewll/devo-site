devo Support

Last updated: May 17, 2026

devo is a daily Bible-reflection journal you share with a few people who actually know you. Write what you read in the Bible and what it’s stirring up in you, then choose who sees it. No public feed, no algorithm. You pick your audience.

How do I get started?

  1. Install devo from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Create an account with your email and a password.
  3. Confirm your email when the verification message arrives.
  4. Add a friend by their handle, or share your invite link.
  5. Tap Compose, write your first reflection, and post it. Friends’ posts unlock once you’ve posted today.

I forgot my password

From the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password” to receive a reset link by email. If the reset email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder or contact us at the email below.

I want to delete my account

See the Delete Account page for the request process and what gets removed.
