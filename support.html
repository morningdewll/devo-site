devo Support

Last updated: May 17, 2026

devo is a daily Bible-reflection journal you share with a few people who actually know you. Write what you read and what it’s stirring up, then pick who sees it. No strangers, no algorithm, no follower count. You choose your audience.

If something below doesn’t answer your question, email support@devo.fyi. I read every message.

How do I get started?

  1. Install devo from the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Create an account with your email and a password.
  3. Confirm your email when the verification message lands in your inbox.
  4. Add a friend by their handle, or send them your invite link.
  5. Tap Compose, write your first reflection, and post it. Your friends’ posts unlock once you’ve posted today.

I forgot my password

From the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password” and a reset link goes to your email. If it doesn’t show up within a few minutes, check your spam folder. Still nothing? Email the address at the bottom of this page and I’ll get you back in.

Something is broken

Email support@devo.fyi with a short description of what happened and a screenshot if you can grab one. I read every report and reply to most of them within a day or two.

I want to delete my account

See the Delete Account page for the request process and what gets removed.
