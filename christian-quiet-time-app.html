Quiet time is simple: a verse, a few unhurried minutes, something honest written down. Keeping it daily is the hard part. devo is a quiet time app built around the one thing that makes a habit hold: people. You write your reflection on today’s verse first, then your friends’ reflections unlock. The app is literally named devo: quiet time.

Why solo quiet time fades

A solo quiet time runs on willpower, and willpower has bad weeks. You miss a morning, then a few, and there’s no one in it with you to notice. The habit doesn’t end with a decision. It just quietly stops.

What a shared quiet time looks like

You don’t need to be in the same room or on a call. Each person reads the verse and writes a few honest lines whenever their day allows. Then you read each other’s, the same day. Your quiet time stays quiet and personal. It’s the showing up that’s shared.

In devo the gate holds it together: you post your own reflection first, and that unlocks your friends’. Nobody lurks, nobody performs. It’s a post-first devotional.

The tree you grow together

Your streak is a little tree that grows on the days you and your friends show up. Miss one day, it’s fine. Miss two in a row and it withers, but it doesn’t vanish to zero. The withered tree stays in your past trees, so the season you had is remembered, not erased. More on that: a streak without the guilt.

Built for quiet time, not engagement

No public feed, no follower count, no algorithm deciding what you see. You pick who reads each reflection: just you, your friends, or a group you choose. And it’s free, with no ads, no paywall, no subscription.

Common questions

What is a quiet time app?
A quiet time app gives your daily Bible reading and reflection one place to live: today’s verse, your written reflection, and your history. devo adds friends to that: you post your reflection first, then your friends’ unlock.

Can you do quiet time with friends?
Yes. In devo each person reads and writes on their own time, then reads each other’s that day. The gate keeps it honest: nobody can read without writing their own first.

Is devo free?
Yes. devo is free, with no ads, no paywall, no subscription.
