devo is what Christian social media looks like when you take out the parts that make social media exhausting. There’s no public feed, no follower count, and no algorithm deciding what you see. Just your friends’ reflections on today’s verse, which unlock after you’ve written your own.

A public feed changes what you write

The moment strangers can read your reflection, you start writing for them. The honest sentence gets traded for the impressive one. That’s not a character flaw, it’s what an audience does. devo removes the audience: what you write goes to people you chose, who are writing too.

What private means here

You pick the audience every time you post: just you, your friends, or a group you made, like your church small group or your roommates. Your reflections stay off your profile unless you choose to add them. And your friends only see today’s reflection if they’ve written their own, because devo is a post-first devotional.

No algorithm, on purpose

Nothing in devo is ranked, recommended, or boosted. There’s no discovery tab, no trending, no number telling you how you performed. You see your friends’ reflections in order, when they post them. The app has nothing to optimize, because there’s no ad slot to fill: devo is free, with no ads, and your data isn’t for sale.

Social, in the older sense

devo is still social media, technically. You post, your friends react and comment, a streak grows. But it’s social the way a dinner table is social, not the way a stadium is. A few people who actually know you, showing up for the same practice, reading the same verse.

Common questions

Is there a private Christian social media app?
Yes. devo is a private place to share Bible reflections with your friends, or a group you choose. There’s no public feed, no follower count, and no algorithm. Your friends’ reflections unlock after you post your own.

Does devo have an algorithm or a public feed?
No. Nothing is ranked, recommended, or surfaced by engagement. You see your friends’ reflections in order, when they post them, and only after you’ve posted yours. Nothing you write is public unless you choose to put it on your profile.

Is devo free?
Yes. devo is free, with no ads, no paywall, no subscription.
