How it works

  1. Read today’s verse. Write what you noticed.
    A small daily journal. Yours alone, until you choose to share it with your group.
  2. Your friends’ reflections show up after yours does.
    No silent reading. You write first, then you get to read what your closest friends wrote.
  3. Your tree grows on the days you show up.
    One missed day is fine. Two in a row and your tree withers. The old one stays in your archive.
