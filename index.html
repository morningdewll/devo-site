How it works

  1. Open today’s verse, write what it stirs in you.
    A small daily journal. Yours alone, until you choose to share.
  2. Your friend circle unlocks — only after yours.
    No silent lurking. Read your closest friends’ reflections after writing your own.
  3. Your tree grows each day you show up.
    Grace covers the days you miss. Miss too many and a new tree begins. The old one stays in your archive.
