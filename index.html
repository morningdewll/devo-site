Common questions

What is devo?

devo is a daily Bible-reflection journal you share with your friends. Write what today’s verse is stirring in you, then pick who sees it: just you, your friends, or a group you choose. No strangers and no algorithm.

Is devo free?

Yes. devo is free, with no ads, no paywall, no subscription.

Why can’t I see my friends’ reflections until I post my own?

It’s the rule devo is built on: write first, read second. Post today’s reflection and your friends’ unlock. They can’t read yours until they’ve written one either. The gate works the same for everyone.

What happens if I miss a day?

One missed day is fine and the tree keeps growing. Miss two days in a row and the tree withers. Plant a new seed and start again. The withered tree stays in your past trees, so the season you had is remembered, not erased.
