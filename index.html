You’ve probably tried both

Posting for strangers

Put a verse on your story and it goes to everyone: old classmates, coworkers, people you barely know. Somewhere in there it stops being reflection and starts being performance.

Journaling alone

Start a private journal and it works, for a while. Then a busy week hits, nobody notices you stopped, and the journal doesn’t ask where you went.

devo is a free post-first devotional app: you write your reflection on today’s verse first, then your closest friends’ reflections unlock. No public feed, no followers, no algorithm.

How it works

  1. Read today’s verse. Write what you noticed.
    A small daily journal. Yours alone, until you choose to share it with your friends or a group.
  2. Your friends’ reflections show up after yours does.
    No silent reading. You write first, then you get to read what your closest friends wrote.
  3. Your tree grows on the days you show up.
    One missed day is fine. Two in a row and your tree withers. It stays in your past trees, remembered, not erased.

Common questions

What is devo?
devo is a daily Bible-reflection journal you share with your friends. Write what today’s verse is stirring in you, then pick who sees it: just you, your friends, or a group you choose. No strangers and no algorithm.
Is devo free?
Yes. devo is free, with no ads, no paywall, no subscription.
Why can’t I see my friends’ reflections until I post my own?
It’s the rule devo is built on: write first, read second. Post today’s reflection and your friends’ unlock. They can’t read yours until they’ve written one either. The gate works the same for everyone.
What happens if I miss a day?
One missed day is fine and the tree keeps growing. Miss two days in a row and the tree withers. Plant a new seed and start again. The withered tree stays in your past trees, so the season you had is remembered, not erased.
