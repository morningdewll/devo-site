A post-first devotional is one you can’t lurk on. You read today’s verse and write your own reflection first. That unlocks your friends’. No posting, no peeking. devo is the free app built on it: read the Bible with your closest friends, write before you read, and grow it together.

How a post-first devotional works

  1. Read today’s verse. Write what you noticed.
    A small daily journal. Yours alone, until you share it with your group.
  2. Post yours. Your friends’ reflections unlock.
    Post to unlock. No silent reading. You write first, then you read what your closest friends wrote.
  3. Your tree grows on the days you show up.
    One missed day is fine. Two in a row and it withers. It stays in your past trees, remembered, not erased.

Why post-first beats lurking

Most ways of “sharing faith” online let you watch and never show up. You scroll everyone’s verse graphics, screenshot a sermon, sit quiet in the group chat, and never open your own Bible. A post-first devotional closes that door. You only see your friends after you’ve shown up yourself.

The gate is the point. It works the same for everyone: write first, read second. So the people in your group are reading the Bible alongside you, not lurking.

A streak you grow together. When it withers, you keep it

devo has a streak. It’s a little tree that grows every day you and your friends show up. Miss one day, it’s fine. Miss two in a row and it withers. But it doesn’t vanish to zero. It gets planted in your past trees, so the season you had is remembered, not erased.

And you don’t grow it alone. The tree only moves when your people show up too.

Your reflections stay yours. You choose who sees each one: just you, your friends, or a group you made. And you can add the ones you want to your profile.

Is this a Christian version of BeReal?

Kind of. People call devo “the Christian BeReal” because of the post-to-see-others part. (devo isn’t affiliated with BeReal.) But BeReal is a photo of your random Tuesday. devo is your time in the Word, with your friends, and it keeps your history instead of disappearing it.
